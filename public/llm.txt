# ipo-premium

> Live Indian IPO Grey Market Premium (GMP), real-time BSE & NSE subscription tracking, allotment status verification, and primary market analytics.

ipo-premium.com provides real-time, verified data on Indian Mainboard and SME Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and National Stock Exchange (NSE).

## Core Market Trackers

- [IPO GMP Today](https://ipo-premium.com/ipo-gmp): Live Grey Market Premium (GMP), estimated listing price, and expected listing gain percentages for active Mainboard and SME IPOs.
- [Live IPO Subscription Status](https://ipo-premium.com/subscription): Real-time subscription tracking across QIB, NII (HNI), and Retail investor quotas with cumulative BSE/NSE bidding metrics.
- [IPO Allotment Status Hub](https://ipo-premium.com/allotment): Comprehensive share allocation verification across all RTAs and stock exchanges.
- [Live Ongoing IPOs](https://ipo-premium.com/ipo/live): List of all currently open public issues accepting bids.
- [Upcoming IPOs 2026](https://ipo-premium.com/ipo/upcoming): Comprehensive tracker for upcoming IPOs, tentative issue dates, price bands, issue sizes, and SEBI DRHP approvals.
- [Listed IPO Performance](https://ipo-premium.com/ipo/listed): Historical listing day gains, current trading prices, and post-listing performance.

## Official Registrar Allotment Checkers

- [Link Intime IPO Allotment Status](https://ipo-premium.com/allotment/link-intime): Official Link Intime India (MUFG Intime) direct portal and mirror servers for Mainboard IPOs.
- [KFintech IPO Allotment Status](https://ipo-premium.com/allotment/kfintech): Direct access to KFin Technologies allotment servers (Server 1 to 5) for high-traffic issues.
- [Bigshare Services IPO Allotment Status](https://ipo-premium.com/allotment/bigshare): Check BSE SME and NSE Emerge IPO application status by PAN.
- [BSE India IPO Application Check](https://ipo-premium.com/allotment/bse-india): Centralized official Bombay Stock Exchange allotment verification.
- [Skyline Financial IPO Allotment Status](https://ipo-premium.com/allotment/skyline): Official Skyline Financial Services allocation portal for SME public issues.
